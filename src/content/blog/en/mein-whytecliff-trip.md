I’d heard that Whytecliff Park was supposed to be really beautiful, so I thought I’d go and have a look. I packed a book and some muesli and hopped on a bus towards Horseshoe Bay on a bit of a whim. From there, I planned to walk on to Whytecliff Beach and maybe a lookout too.

I’d taken my time getting out the door. That meant I was leaving later than planned, but getting to the water in time for sunset sounded pretty good too. At least that was a convincing enough explanation for my late start.

## A look around Horseshoe Bay first

In Horseshoe Bay, I briefly considered getting an ice cream. I could always treat myself to one later. To get my little hike started, I picked up a Powerade and set off. Along the way, I passed a signpost showing the distances to all sorts of cities. It was a little reassuring that my actual destination was pretty close by comparison.

![Several directional signs with city names and distances on a post beside a sidewalk](<../../../assets/photos/mein-whytecliff-trip/WhatsApp Image 2026-11-21 at 13.22.49 (1).jpeg>)

_I wasn’t planning to go quite that far today. My own photo._

I stopped by the bay for a moment too. In front of me were the water and the mountains, with trees along the shore. Once again, it was very easy to take my time before moving on.

![View across a grassy waterfront area in Horseshoe Bay towards the water and mountains under a blue sky](<../../../assets/photos/mein-whytecliff-trip/WhatsApp Image 2026-11-21 at 13.22.49.jpeg>)

_Even the starting point in Horseshoe Bay looked pretty good. My own photo._

## I could actually stay here

I carried on towards Nelson Avenue. Near Batchelor Bay Park, a little footpath branched off to the side, and I decided to see where it went. I was still planning to get to Whytecliff Beach. Maybe that lookout too. But then I found myself by the water, with the sun shining and everything feeling pretty quiet.

I looked around and thought, why keep going? It was already really lovely right here. So I found a place to sit and took out my book. Just like that, my spontaneous trip had found its destination.

![An open book in front of the water, with the low sun shining above the wooded shore](<../../../assets/photos/mein-whytecliff-trip/WhatsApp Image 2026-11-21 at 13.22.50 (2).jpeg>)

_The rest of the walk could wait while I read a few pages with this view. My own photo._

I ate the muesli I’d brought, read for a bit and kept looking up at the water. I’d been sipping my Powerade along the way, so I was pretty well equipped for my little break. There wasn’t much else I wanted to do at that moment.

As the sun sank lower, the light across the bay changed. I put my book aside again and took a few photos. The horizon slowly turned orange, while the water in front of me seemed to grow calmer. I was pretty glad I’d been curious about that little turnoff.

![View over driftwood on the shore towards a calm bay between wooded rocks in the evening light](<../../../assets/photos/mein-whytecliff-trip/WhatsApp Image 2026-11-21 at 13.22.51 (1).jpeg>)

_Another look across the bay before heading back. My own photo._

## A quick stop on the way back

Eventually, I packed up my things and wandered back. I passed Tantalus Park and wondered whether there might be something else to discover there. Beyond the entrance, there was mostly a lawn. For a moment, I’d thought I’d found another little destination, but it turned out to be a very short visit.

![A Tantalus Park sign beside a yellow gate and a lawn surrounded by trees](<../../../assets/photos/mein-whytecliff-trip/WhatsApp Image 2026-11-21 at 13.22.52.jpeg>)

_Conveniently, the next park was right on my way back. My own photo._

It was getting darker along the streets, and I headed back towards the bus. I hadn’t actually made it to Whytecliff Beach that day. But I’d found a quiet place by the water, eaten my muesli and sat with a book in the evening sun. For a spontaneous trip, I thought that was pretty good.
